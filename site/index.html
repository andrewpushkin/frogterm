frogterm

Turns a Seeed reTerminal E1002 — an ESP32-S3 with a 7.3″ six-colour E Ink panel — into a family photo frame with today's events and the weather. It runs for months on its battery.

What it shows

Photos

A random photo from a shared Google Photos album, with the date it was taken. Portrait photos come in pairs.

Calendar

Today's events from your Google calendars, tomorrow's once today is over. Latin, Cyrillic and Hebrew.

Weather

Now and every two hours up to +12 h, with rain chance or the UV index, from Open-Meteo.

Battery life

Three updates a day and deep sleep in between. Most updates need no Wi-Fi: photos are kept ready in flash.

Install

From this page

Connect the reTerminal with a USB-C cable and use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

This browser cannot flash devices: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure page (https).

Updating keeps the device's settings, unless you choose to erase the device when asked.

From the frame's settings page

Once frogterm runs on the frame, later versions need no cable: download firmware.bin from the latest release, open the frame's settings page (hold green 10 s) and install it in the Firmware box. Settings and photos stay; a version that fails is rolled back by itself.

With espflash

Download from the latest release:

espflash write-bin 0x0 firmware-full.bin && espflash erase-region 0xa10000 0x2000   # first install (erases the settings)
espflash write-bin 0x10000 firmware.bin && espflash erase-region 0xa10000 0x2000    # update (keeps the settings)

Set it up

  1. A newly installed frame has no settings, so it starts in settings mode. The panel shows the network frogterm-setup and the address http://192.168.4.1/.
  2. Join that Wi-Fi network with your phone and open the address.
  3. Fill in the album link, the city, your calendars and your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, then press Save.
  4. Press the green button on the frame. It joins your Wi-Fi and shows its first photo.

To change a setting later, hold the green button for 10 seconds (the frame's own network) or the right button for 10 seconds (the page on your Wi-Fi, at the address on the panel). The Wi-Fi password and secret calendar links are never shown on the page.

Where to find the album link and the calendar addresses: the README.
